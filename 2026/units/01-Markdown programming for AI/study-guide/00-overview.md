# Unit 01: Markdown Programming for AI -- Overview

**Unit Code**: AI 100
**Estimated Time**: 2 hours
**Difficulty**: Foundational

---

## Why This Unit Exists

The USAAIO Round 1 paper-based component requires contestants to write solutions in Google Colab text (markdown) cells. You will need to:

1. **Format written responses** clearly using headings, lists, bold/italic, and tables
2. **Include code snippets** with proper syntax highlighting and annotations
3. **Typeset mathematical formulas** using LaTeX -- from simple fractions to full attention mechanisms

This is not a "nice to have." If you cannot typeset your answer, you cannot communicate it. A correct solution that the grader cannot read is a zero.

---

## Prerequisites

- None. This is the first unit in the USAAIO curriculum.
- Access to [Google Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/) is required for practice.

## How This Unit Connects to USAAIO

Every subsequent unit in the curriculum builds on these skills:

- **Unit 02 (Math Foundations)**: You will typeset eigendecompositions, matrix operations, and probability distributions
- **Unit 04 (Supervised Learning)**: You will write loss function derivations and gradient computations
- **Unit 07 (Deep Learning)**: You will present backpropagation derivations with aligned equations
- **Unit 09 (Transformers)**: You will typeset the full attention formula and multi-head attention

On the actual USAAIO exam, roughly 30-40% of Round 1 questions require mathematical typesetting. Students who cannot write LaTeX fluently lose significant time.

---

## How to Use This Unit

1. **Read** each study guide section in order (01, 02, 03)
2. **Complete** the corresponding exercises after each section
3. **Submit** the assignment notebooks as your final check
4. **Keep** the cheat sheet (`cheat-sheet.md`) open during practice -- you will memorize the syntax through use

The LaTeX math section (03) is by far the most important. Spend the majority of your time there.
